  • StupidparticleVeteran chain founded 2013 (Taipei / Hsinchu / Taichung); 500k+ visitors, known for 'Deadly Chains' and 'Deja Vu'. Approx. Taipei.
  • EnterSpace'Escape-room cinema' with film-grade effects, ~14 themes, 2–10 players. Zhongshan, Taipei (approx.).
  • ClubonPioneer of outdoor 'issue-based reality games' since 2016, 40+ titles linking social topics. Taipei (approx.).
  • SlipawayEscape blended with immersive theater; a 100-ping double-height set with two pro actors per session. Shilin, Taipei (approx.).
  • Dream Kingdom'Dream-entering' ritual with horror/mystery/idol/family styles; runs the puzzle-free immersive show INSANE. Dadaocheng, Taipei (approx.).
  • FunlockWell-known chain that grew from the north and expanded to Taichung. Taipei (approx.).
  • LOOPA Taichung flagship — three branches clustered on Wenxin Rd., known for varied horror themes. Taichung (approx.).
  • BGLOCKReasoning-focused studio; original branch plus a store near Miyahara. East/Central Taichung (approx.).
  • SoGoodsNovel-like visuals with a strong 'storytelling' immersion style. Taichung (approx.).
  • Mr.MythBrought China-style light/sound + live-NPC horror to Taichung in 2024 (e.g. the 'Haunted Toilet' series). Taichung (approx.).
  • Rostart GamesScenery-rich adventure rooms; the long-running pirate hit 'One-eyed Jack'. Xinyi, Taipei (approx.).
  • Crazy MiImmersive light/sound with live dance performance; the Japanese-yokai bathhouse room 'Yotang'. Zhonghe, New Taipei (approx.).
  • A5 StudioTaoyuan's first escape studio; theatre/film crew built theatre-grade sets. Taoyuan + Zhongli (approx.).
  • Grimm TellA Hsinchu must-play with rich storylines and beginner-friendly rooms like 'Moon-Thief's Notes'. Hsinchu (approx.).
  • TAOG (God's Absence)Self-styled 'Taiwan's largest', spanning Taichung / Tainan / Taipei; e.g. 'Heir of Fairy Tales'. Central Taichung (approx.).
  • EscerFounded by enthusiasts; 20+ big themes including VR and immersive theatre, three branches, 2-player start. Taichung (approx.).
  • In-meEerie micro-horror based on true events; debut 'Lingering Grudge' (2024) casts you as a coroner. Gushan, Kaohsiung (approx.).
  • One's PlanEscape + script-murder with detailed storytelling; horror 'Inner Demon' and emotional 'Room 37'. Zuoying, Kaohsiung (approx.).
  • Mr. BoomKaohsiung's most-visited, open latest (to midnight), great value. Sanmin, Kaohsiung (approx.).
  • Plot-in-PlotA 'museum' world of many story rooms (e.g. 'Ghost Scheme'), since 2022. Tainan (approx.).
