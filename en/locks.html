Lock Families

16 kinds of locks and triggers you will crack — what they look like, how they open, and which clue feeds each one.

Combination

6
  • lockNumber Combination LockVery commonRows of 0–9 dials; line up the right digits and pull.
  • lockRotary Dial LockMediumA single dial; turn right-left-right to three numbers.
  • lockLetter / Word LockMedium-highWheels of A–Z; spell the right word to open.
  • lockLuggage / Briefcase LockMedium-highA small 3-wheel dial built into a case; set code, press to pop.
  • lockCryptexMediumA cylinder with letter rings; spell the word to pull out the core.
  • lockKana / Zhuyin / Symbol LockMediumDecode symbols to kana, Zhuyin or numbers, then feed a standard lock.

Key locks

1
  • lockKey PadlockVery commonThe classic lock — find the matching key and turn.

Directional

1
  • lockDirectional LockMediumOpen with a sequence of up/down/left/right, not numbers.

Electronic

3
  • lockElectromagnetic Lock (Maglock)Very commonElectric magnet holds a door shut; correct input cuts power to release.
  • lockRFID / Proximity CardMediumTap the right tagged object to a reader to trigger a release.
  • lockDigital Keypad LockMedium-highType the right code on an electronic pad to release a maglock.

Mechanical

3
  • lockMechanical Mechanism LockMediumGears, levers and button sequences release a latch physically.
  • lockHidden Magnet LockMediumFind the right magnet and place it to release a latch.
  • lockWeight / IR Sensor TriggerMediumPressure plates, lasers or proximity sensors fire a mechanism.

Hidden / clue

2
  • lockHidden Compartment / Secret DrawerVery commonClues hidden in false bottoms or disguised objects.
  • lockUV / Blacklight ClueVery commonNot a lock — invisible-ink clues that only show under UV light.
