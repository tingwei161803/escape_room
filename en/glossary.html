Glossary & Lingo

34 term(s)

Game Master (GM)expand_more
Staff who greet players, explain rules, monitor the game, give hints and keep things safe and on-track.
NPCexpand_more
Non-player character — staff who play a role and interact with players to push the story (e.g. a hotel manager).
Little Angel (xiao tianshi)expand_more
Taiwan nickname for the GM / host — tells the opening story, gives hints when you are stuck, and leads you out if needed.
Clueexpand_more
An element inside the game scene that players must observe and combine. Different from a 'hint'.
Hintexpand_more
Help that comes from outside the game, given by the GM. In Taiwan often counted as 'hint tokens' or a hint quota.
Mechanismexpand_more
An interactive device triggered by circuits, sensors or physical mechanics; meeting its condition opens a door or fires an effect.
Hidden compartmentexpand_more
A concealed layer, false bottom or disguised object that hides clues or keys.
Stuckexpand_more
When the team is blocked on a puzzle and cannot progress — usually the cue to ask for a hint.
Combination lockexpand_more
A lock opened by entering the correct code — numeric, letter or directional.
Linear vs non-linearexpand_more
Linear = puzzles solved in a fixed order, one after another; non-linear = many puzzles can be tackled at once, in any order.
Immersiveexpand_more
Design — set, story, characters — that makes players feel like part of the story.
Script murder (LARP)expand_more
A story-driven, role-play-and-deduction game type, often listed alongside escape rooms.
Red herringexpand_more
A fake clue or prop that leads nowhere and only wastes the team's time.
Deductionexpand_more
A puzzle requiring deductive, logical reasoning to solve.
Escape rateexpand_more
The share of teams that successfully escape — often used to signal difficulty.
Timerexpand_more
Completing the goal within a limit (commonly 60 minutes); a countdown timer is standard.
Propexpand_more
An in-game object — could be a puzzle component, or a red herring.
Meta / final puzzleexpand_more
A final puzzle that combines the results of earlier ones, usually near the end.
Gatingexpand_more
A design technique that blocks access to a space or element until a specific puzzle is solved.
Resetexpand_more
Restoring the room to its starting state before the next team — re-hiding props, re-locking, rebooting electronics.
Brute forceexpand_more
Solving without reasoning by trying every possibility of one digit/combination — handy when you are one digit short.
Tongling (mind-reading)expand_more
Taiwan slang: guessing the right answer with no logic, or a puzzle whose logic is too big a leap to follow.
Sightseeing (guanguang)expand_more
Taiwan slang for admiring the set and mechanisms without actually solving and progressing.
Hamster / Tankexpand_more
Horror-room slang: a 'hamster' scares easily; a 'tank' is fearless and shields teammates up front.
Private session (baochang)expand_more
Booking takes the whole session — you are never grouped with strangers, the Taiwan norm.
Spoiler / spoiler-freeexpand_more
'Spoiler' = revealing puzzles or the ending; a 'spoiler-free' review shares without ruining the game.
Golden Escape Awardexpand_more
Taiwan's annual selection/awards for real-life games.
TPC clubexpand_more
Taiwan's largest escape-room player community (the 'T.P.C. real-life escape club' on Facebook).
Mechanism loverexpand_more
A player who favours mechanism-heavy rooms (sensors, circuits) over traditional padlocks.
Linear vs sandboxexpand_more
Design terms: 'linear' = puzzles in fixed order; 'sandbox' (non-linear) = many threads run at once, good for splitting up.
Immersive theatreexpand_more
Experience-driven rooms with close actor interaction and strong story immersion; some have no puzzles.
Bao (a dud)expand_more
Player slang for a disappointing room — weak puzzles, story or production.
Walkthrough (peek)expand_more
Reading the solution to a room — a major spoiler that ruins the one-time experience; avoid before playing.
Clear timeexpand_more
How long a team took to escape; bragging rights and a difficulty signal alongside escape rate.
